WHY INTERFERE

The problem I’ve been solving—moved closer to the product.

I have spent my career finding failures hidden between systems, definitions, data, and handoffs before they become business failures. Interfere applies that instinct directly to software: detect what users experience, diagnose what actually caused it, and close the loop with a verified fix.

That symptom → signal → root cause → fix → verification chain is exactly the kind of system I want to go deep on. I would bring an operator’s attention to edge cases, an analyst’s demand for evidence, and a builder’s willingness to create whatever is missing.

WHAT I WOULD EXPLORE

  3. 03Closed-loop verification that proves a fix resolves the original failure without introducing a new one.
